# PropertyOS Founding Broker Program — Operating Manual

## The only opening pitch

> I work with Pune brokers who send property photos and details separately on WhatsApp. PropertyOS turns the same material into one professional branded page in about a minute and shows when buyers engage. Give me one property and I will build it with you now—free and without commitment.

Do not lead with CRM, AI, automation, or “operating system.” Demonstrate one real property before explaining the broader product.

## Territory sequence

1. Baner–Balewadi–Wakad: stay until 10 retained brokers.
2. Kharadi–Viman Nagar: add only after the first territory meets the gate.
3. Adjacent Pune territory: add after 15 total paying Pune brokers.
4. Thane or Navi Mumbai: choose one after Pune produces a repeatable demo-to-paid motion.

## Lifecycle definitions

| Stage | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Lead | Named broker plus phone or verified profile in a selected micro-market |
| Contacted | Two-way call, message, or in-person conversation |
| Demo completed | One real property was created and shared during the session |
| Pilot | Workspace created with 14-day pilot dates |
| Activated | Branding complete, 3 listings published, and 1 real share action |
| Retained | Meaningful product activity in 3 of the first 4 weeks |
| Paying | Payment received and plan marked active |
| Advocate | Testimonial, case study, or qualified referral supplied |

Never advance a lead based on intention alone. Record the evidence and date.

## First 14 days

- Build 300 qualified leads in `broker-pipeline-template.csv`; do not buy generic databases.
- Interview 30 brokers before treating messaging as final.
- Recruit 10 design partners and observe them using their own phones.
- Prepare 3 demo listings, a 45-second demo, a 3-minute walkthrough, and English/Hindi/Marathi voice notes.
- Launch acquisition only when signup → first listing → share → buyer view → lead → dashboard works end-to-end.

### Interview guide

1. Show me how you received and shared the last property.
2. Where do you keep photos, owner details, price changes, and availability?
3. What do buyers ask again after you send a property?
4. How do you know whether a buyer viewed or forwarded it?
5. Which tools have you tried, and when did you stop using them?
6. If today’s demonstrated workflow continued after the pilot, what would make ₹499/month worthwhile?

Capture exact phrases, not summaries. Do not pitch until question five is complete.

## Daily field cadence

Tuesday–Thursday target: 8 visits, 6 calls, 4 personalized WhatsApp introductions, and 2 community/referral introductions per day. Log the outcome before starting the next contact.

After a demo:

> Your page is ready. Send it to five genuine prospects today. Tomorrow we will review the views and enquiries together. If it does not make sharing easier, you do not need to continue.

On conversion day:

> During the pilot you published {listings}, shared {shares} times, and received {buyer_actions} buyer actions. Continuing is ₹499/month, including branding, analytics, brochures, AI copy, and assisted support.

## Weekly scorecard

Every Monday record:

- Leads added, contacted, demos, pilots, activated, retained, paid, advocates.
- Contacted→demo, demo→pilot, pilot→activated, activated→retained, activated→paid.
- Median time to first listing and first share.
- Publishing failures and support requests by category.
- Acquisition cost by source and territory.
- Interviews completed, exact objections, and product blockers.

Decision gates:

- Under 40% pilot activation: pause new acquisition and fix onboarding.
- Under 50% four-week retention among activated brokers: revisit the daily habit/value proposition.
- Retained brokers reject ₹499: review demonstrated value before changing price.
- Do not run broad paid advertising until one channel has under three-month payback.

## Events and referrals

Run a weekly 45-minute “Broker Digital Clinic.” Each attendee brings one property and leaves with a live page. The agenda is 5-minute problem framing, 10-minute demonstration, 20-minute assisted build, 5-minute sharing exercise, and 5-minute pilot invitation.

For the 3 Broker Circle, issue the in-product referral link. Grant one free month only after one referred broker becomes paid. Partner commissions are likewise paid on activation or payment, never raw leads.

## Case-study evidence

Record the broker’s previous workflow, time to publish before/after, listings and shares in the pilot, buyer views/actions/leads, one direct quote, and permission level (private sales proof, named written case study, or public video). Never invent results or publish without explicit permission.
